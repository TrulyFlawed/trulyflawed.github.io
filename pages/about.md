# About me

Hiya, I'm Duskfall, a trans woman who grew up in the Midwest region of the United States and has indigenous ancestry.

## Interests

Over the years I've collected "hobbies" and interests to hoard for a page like this someday.

- **Open-source and open-knowledge**: I love the ethos of these communities as providing valuable things for everyone to use at no cost. Information and programs should be shared creations for everyone to benefit from.
- **Programming**: I designed and created this very site myself, so obviously I'm into programming. I'm not super experienced but tinkering around with code and making cool stuff is fun.
- **Note-taking**: Or knowledge management, whatever fits. Just the practice of working with information by writing it down, organizing it, and refining it is something that deeply interests me. I like to use Obsidian as my note-taking app.
- **Art and writing**: I like creating things because it is fun. It's a valuable tool for expression and just seems like a nice skill to have nowadays when it seems so few care for craft.

## History

From a young age I drew art, read books, and played video games. A middle school geography class opened me to the world of social sciences like history and grand strategy games like Hearts of Iron IV.

Hearts of Iron IV modding was my first "real" experience with programming, which then later developed further as I got into web development and started projects like this website and those listed in my [portfolio](/portfolio/).

In mid-2025 I discovered the note-taking app Obsidian, which began my interest in writing and knowledge management. It also revived my interest in art, which had stagnated for a long time.
